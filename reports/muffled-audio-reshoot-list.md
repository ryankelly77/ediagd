# Muffled audio — reshoot list

30 films profiled end to end, out of 228 published masters.

**Measure.** HF ratio = 10·log10( energy 4–8 kHz / energy 0.3–4 kHz ), on voiced
frames only. A lapel under fabric low-passes speech: consonants live 4–8 kHz,
vowels below 1 kHz, so occlusion strips the top and the ear hears "muffled".

**Method.** 2.5 s buckets, median of the voiced frames in each; a defect is a run
of ≥2 consecutive buckets below threshold. Thresholds absolute, from the corpus of
228 (median −35.9 dB, σ 4.2): **muffled < −45 dB**, **dull −45 to −41 dB**.

**Validated** against the one film Ryan identified by ear before any measurement:
CAF-002 ranks worst of 228 on window spread, and its worst point (2:09) is the
passage he named.
