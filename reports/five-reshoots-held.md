# The five reshoots: held, and which teaching point is missing in each

**All five are cleaner on audio. All five are held.** Each one drops teaching that
is in the live film and **nowhere else in the library**.

Decided by transcript, as ruled — not by run time, and not by anybody's say-so.

## Method, and the extra step that mattered

Both transcripts exist, so each live film was split into sentences and every
sentence scored against the incoming film. A sentence carrying less than 30% of
its content words into the new take is a candidate for "dropped".

**Then the candidates were checked against the nine-part *Get the Hell Out of
Here* series**, because both the old and new films end by pointing at it — so a
shorter new film could be deliberate de-duplication rather than loss. That check
changes the answer for several sentences and is the reason it was run:
*"I'll come find you"*, *ambush vs reservation* and the *quick-lane lounge*
framing **do** appear in the speech series, so they are not lost.

What follows survived both filters: absent from the new take **and** absent from
the speech series.

## 1 · ACO-055 — At the Kiosk 273s → 158s (42% shorter)

Audio: 21.4% muffled → 0.0%. 730 → 444 words, 16 orphaned sentences.

**Missing:**
- **There is no factory interval for an AC odor treatment.** *"No manufacturer can
  publish a number. There's no reminder on the dash, no maintenance schedule."*
- **"If you don't offer it, nobody does"** — and the customer drives another year
  with nobody mentioning it. This is the film's argument for why the stage exists.
- **The annual anchor** — *"the happy birthday, once a year and ideally before it
  gets hot"*, with the note that annual is hard for customers to hold on to, so
  give them a reason to remember.
- The quick-lane kiosk framing: in a drop-off shop the customer hands over keys
  and leaves; here they are standing in front of you.

**The new take is not a cut of the old one.** It teaches the *three levels of odor
treatment* — scented wafers, the aerosol evaporator disinfectant, the wand-in fog
service — plus "have demos done on your own vehicle". None of that is in the live
film. These are two different films about the same op code and stage.

## 2 · ACO-055 — MPI Setup 264s → 108s (59% shorter)

Audio: 21.9% muffled → 0.0%. 665 → 316 words, 13 orphaned sentences. The deepest
cut of the five.

**Missing:**
- **"Two words that aren't in there: oil change. We do not do oil changes. We do
  5,000 mile services."** With the reason: *"An oil change is a commodity somebody
  buys on price. A 5,000 mile service is a scheduled event that happens with you."*
- **"Never say the customer is waiting. Waiting is something you do at the DMV.
  It's time being taken from someone."**
- The Walmart differentiation — *"Somebody can get their oil changed at Walmart,
  but this experience only happens here… they cannot get a certified technician
  putting eyes on 27 items anywhere."*

All three are absent from the incoming film and from all nine speech films. The
first two are named, quotable rules about vocabulary, which is the kind of thing
this library exists to teach.

## 3 · ACO-055 — On the Drive 223s → 153s (31% shorter)

Audio: 10.8% muffled → 0.0%. 593 → 439 words, 13 orphaned sentences.

Both films teach *don't sell it on smell*, so the core survives. What does not:

- **The explicit don'ts** — *"Don't ask if they've noticed an odor. Don't sniff
  when you open the door."*
- **The reason** — *"When you tell somebody their car smells bad, what they hear is
  they smell bad."*
- The framing question the film is built around: *"So how do you sell an odor
  service without mentioning odor?"*

The new take adds the cowl observation — leaves by the dash during the
walk-around — which the live film does not have.

## 4 · CAF-002 — MPI Setup 158s → 98s (38% shorter)

Audio: 16.4% muffled → 0.0%. 468 → 278 words, 6 orphaned sentences.

**Missing, and it is this film's whole argument:**
- **"A dirty cabin air filter photographs better than almost anything else on that
  car."** With the supporting detail — *leaves, pine needles, sometimes whole seed
  pods* — and the conclusion *"it looks exactly as bad as it is, and there's no

- *"That filter lives behind the glove box on most vehicles. You weren't pulling it
  on the drive, and I told you not to even try, because it's time consuming."*

The new take is the standard setup speech with the cabin air filter swapped into
the third slot. It is a good speech; it is not this lesson.

## 5 · CAF-002 — After-MPI 228s → 162s (29% shorter)

Audio: 16.4% muffled → 0.0%. 665 → 468 words, 8 orphaned sentences.

The closest of the five — the Mr. Smith speech is intact in both. Missing:

- **The worked total** — the live film prices the recommendation and reaches a
  named figure, *$268.58*, alongside the oil change and tire rotation. The new take
  has no worked number.
- *"Even with the photo in front of them, even if that filter looks terrible, show
  them the debris."*

## What this is not

**This is not "Mitch made worse films."** Four of the five teach something the live
film does not, and all five are audibly cleaner — the profiler found these op
codes, the page named them, he shot them, and the audio problem is genuinely
fixed. The loop worked.

They are **differently scoped films**, and they look rebuilt around the new
nine-part speech series: the generic setup speech has moved there, which is why
the new takes are shorter. That is a sound restructuring.

But the quick-lane-specific teaching went with it, and it did not land anywhere.
Replacing the live films would trade a muffled recording of content we have for a
clean recording of content we don't.

## The options, none of which is mine to pick

1. **Publish both** — the new takes as separate films rather than replacements.
   Nothing is lost, the op code gets two films at that stage, and the muffled ones
   stay live until they are re-recorded.
2. **Mitch re-records the missing points** into the new takes, or into the speech
   series where they belong.
3. **Accept the loss** on the points judged not worth keeping — a legitimate call,
   but it should be made on the list above rather than by default.

The files are in the Drop Zone. Nothing is retired, nothing is archived.
